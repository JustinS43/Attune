The driver in the menu photo has a Grove port, so it runs over I²C; the model name printed on the board tells you which library to install. If yours has no Grove port, use D9 for speed and D8 for direction instead. The menu calls the motor brushless, but the photo shows a regular two-wire motor. If yours has three wires, the driver can't run it, so use the menu's Servo Motor on D9 as a tapper instead.

Assembly

About an hour with two people. Test each part on the laptop before taping the next one on.

  1. Clip the webcam over the top of the frame at the bridge, then wrap two strips of tape around the clip and frame. Aim it straight ahead using the laptop preview.
  2. Tape a sound sensor to the outside of each temple, microphone facing out, about 2 cm behind the hinge.
  3. Tape an LED socket on top of each hinge. Bend the LED legs so the LED points back at your eye; the Needle Nose Pliers at the lab's workbench make this easy.
  4. Tape the touch sensor to the right temple where your finger naturally lands.
  5. Wrap a lump of tape off-center on the motor shaft so it wobbles, then tape the motor to the right temple tip.
  6. Run every cable along the temples, with a strip of tape every few centimetres, and let them drop behind your ears. Where each Grove cable ends, push Male to Male jumper wires into its plug and tape the joint.
  7. Build the pack: breadboard, driver board and Arduino side by side, wired per the table. Tape it to your shirt at chest height.
  8. Plug the webcam and the Arduino into the laptop, start the Attune engine, then open the lens view in Chrome.

Notes

Things that will bite on demo day.

  • It's front-heavy, mostly from the webcam. The motor at the back helps balance it; take the rig off between demos.
  • Everything runs from the two USB cables, so stay within reach of the laptop. Keep motor buzzes short, because USB can't run it continuously.
  • Two sound sensors assume the lab lends two. With only one, skip left/right direction; the alerts still work.
  • The Grove cables probably won't reach your chest on their own. The jumpers pushed into their plugs make up the distance; tape each joint so it can't pull apart.
  • Only enroll teammates who agree to it. Strangers stay session-only.
  • Sizes in the model are approximate and depend on the exact parts the lab hands you.
Never set off a real alarm at the venue. Play doorbell and smoke-alarm recordings from the JBL Go instead. The alerts are a demo, not a smoke alarm.
